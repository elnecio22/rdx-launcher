# RDX Launcher

A head unit app for the **2007-2012 Acura RDX**, made for an Android tablet mounted in the dash.

- Live car data from the OBD port (OBDLink EX - USB): speed, rpm, gear, doors, lights, steering, trouble codes explained
- Backup camera with guide lines that follow the steering wheel, parking beeper
- CarPlay (DiPlay) or Android Auto (Open Headunit), full screen or split screen next to the car info
- Maps (Google Maps / Waze / built-in offline map), music, start-up and shut-down films
- Car color: White Diamond Pearl or Crystal Black Pearl

## Install (no computer needed)

1. On the tablet open **Releases** (right side of this page) and tap the newest **RDX-Launcher-x.xx.apk**.
2. When the download finishes, tap it. If Android asks, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for your browser, then tap **Install**.
3. Open **RDX Launcher**. The setup wizard walks you through everything (about 10 minutes).

Updates: the app checks this page by itself and offers **Update** when a new version is out.
Problems: in the app open **Settings > Send a problem report** - it sends the logs automatically.
